# FRAMES Post-Deployment Survey Instrument
## ISO/IEC 25010-Based Evaluation (Student, Faculty, Department Head)

This instrument is designed for post-testing/post-deployment evaluation of FRAMES using the following ISO/IEC 25010 dimensions:

- Functional Suitability
- Performance Efficiency
- Interaction Capability
- Reliability
- Security

## Respondent Groups

- Student respondents: direct system users in pilot classroom operations.
- Faculty respondent: direct instructor user in pilot classroom operations.
- Department Head respondent: direct dashboard/report user.

Because the number of faculty and department head respondents is limited in the one-day pilot, this instrument supports **demo-video-assisted evaluation** for role-specific workflows that are not repeatedly encountered during live operation.

## Response Scale (5-Point Likert)

Use the following scale for every statement:

- 5 = Strongly Agree
- 4 = Agree
- 3 = Neutral
- 2 = Disagree
- 1 = Strongly Disagree

## Interpretation Guide

- 4.21 to 5.00 = Highly Acceptable
- 3.41 to 4.20 = Acceptable
- 2.61 to 3.40 = Moderately Acceptable
- 1.81 to 2.60 = Slightly Acceptable
- 1.00 to 1.80 = Not Acceptable

## Section B: Core ISO/IEC 25010 Items (All Respondents)

### B1. Functional Suitability

1. FRAMES performs the attendance functions needed for my role.
2. The kiosk correctly reflects attendance actions when used as instructed.
3. The dashboard provides the attendance information relevant to my decisions.
4. Exported reports (CSV/PDF) are useful and readable for monitoring.
5. Anomaly notifications are understandable and operationally meaningful.

### B2. Performance Efficiency

6. The kiosk responds within an acceptable time during recognition and logging.
7. Dashboard pages load and refresh fast enough for actual use.
8. Report generation time is acceptable for classroom/department monitoring.
9. The system remains usable during repeated attendance events.
10. Overall, FRAMES performance is sufficient for one-classroom deployment.

### B3. Interaction Capability

11. The interface labels, prompts, and statuses are easy to understand.
12. Kiosk guidance for gesture/action flow is clear.
13. Dashboard navigation is straightforward for my role.
14. Status indicators (e.g., attendance state, alerts) are easy to interpret.
15. The web interface remains usable on mobile-sized screens.

### B4. Reliability

16. Attendance logs appear consistent with actual interactions.
17. The system handles common errors without disrupting workflow.
18. Recorded data remains available after refresh/reload.
19. The kiosk workflow is stable during normal class operation.
20. Overall, I can rely on FRAMES outputs for attendance monitoring.

### B5. Security

21. Access to role-specific features appears properly restricted.
22. Attendance records seem protected from unauthorized changes.
23. The system behavior reduces risk of proxy attendance.
24. The identity + gesture flow improves confidence in attendance validity.
25. I trust FRAMES to handle attendance data responsibly.

## Section C: Role-Specific Items

### C1. Student-Only Items

26. I can easily verify whether my attendance event was successful.
27. My attendance history is clear and useful for self-monitoring.
28. Kiosk prompts help me complete attendance actions correctly.
29. The system reduces confusion compared with manual attendance.
30. I would be willing to use FRAMES regularly in class.

### C2. Faculty-Only Items

31. Class attendance summaries support my classroom management needs.
32. Attendance trend information helps identify at-risk attendance behavior.
33. Report outputs are useful for faculty documentation tasks.
34. The system reduces administrative burden in attendance tracking.
35. I would support continued use of FRAMES in similar classes.

### C3. Department Head-Only Items

36. Department-level views provide useful monitoring information.
37. Aggregated reports support supervisory decision-making.
38. Room/class activity outputs are adequate for oversight.
39. The available data is sufficient for pilot-level evaluation.
40. I would recommend further rollout after iterative improvements.

## Section D: Open-Ended Questions

1. What feature helped you the most during FRAMES use/evaluation?
2. What issue or pain point did you encounter most often?
3. What is your top recommendation before wider deployment?
4. Any additional comments on reliability, usability, or security?

## Scoring Procedure

1. Compute mean score per criterion:
   - Functional Suitability = mean of items 1 to 5
   - Performance Efficiency = mean of items 6 to 10
   - Interaction Capability = mean of items 11 to 15
   - Reliability = mean of items 16 to 20
   - Security = mean of items 21 to 25

2. Compute role-specific means for Section C items.

3. Compute overall acceptability mean:
   - Average of criterion means (B1 to B5)

4. Interpret each mean using the interpretation guide.

## Deployment Note for Limited Evaluators

For this pilot, only one faculty and one department head are direct operational users. To strengthen evidence quality:

- Pair the actual hands-on session with a structured demo video.
- Use the same instrument, marking exposure mode in Section A.
- Clearly separate direct-use findings from demo-assisted findings in analysis.
