We are undergraduate students of the Computer Studies Department at TUP–Manila conducting a capstone study on **FRAMES**, a facial recognition and gesture-gated attendance system deployed on a Raspberry Pi 4 with a USB webcam. **Risks & benefits:** Minimal risk — embeddings cannot be reconstructed into images, and no behavioral surveillance is conducted. Your feedback contributes to the development and evaluation of this academic research.

#### Dear Sir/Ma'am,

We are undergraduate students of the Computer Studies Department at the Technological University of the Philippines–Manila. We are conducting a capstone study titled **"FRAMES: A Web-Based, Gesture-Gated Facial Recognition Attendance System Using Raspberry Pi."** We respectfully invite you to participate as a respondent in the testing and evaluation of the system.

#### Purpose of the Study

The purpose of this study is to design, develop, and evaluate a prototype smart attendance and monitoring system that integrates facial recognition and hand gesture confirmation for real-time, contactless attendance tracking in a classroom setting. The system will be deployed and tested in Room 328 of the College of Science Building using a Raspberry Pi 4 Model B with a USB webcam.

#### Your Participation Will Involve

1. **Face Enrollment** — You will be asked to register your face through the FRAMES web application using a webcam. The system will capture 3–5 frames of your face and convert them into a 512-dimensional numerical embedding. **No actual images or photographs of your face will be stored.**

2. **System Interaction** — During the one-day testing period, you will interact with the system in the following capacity:

   - **As Faculty:** Manage your assigned class through the web dashboard, monitor student attendance, view real-time status of students, and generate attendance reports (CSV/PDF). You will also use the kiosk for your own attendance logging through facial recognition and gesture confirmation.
   - **As Department Head (if applicable):** Access department-level reports, review faculty attendance compliance, and verify faculty accounts through the administrative dashboard.

3. **Demonstration Video** — A comprehensive demonstration video of all FRAMES features will be provided to supplement your hands-on experience, ensuring you can evaluate features across all system modules.

4. **Survey Completion** — After the testing period, you will be asked to complete a survey questionnaire evaluating the system based on the ISO/IEC 25010 Software Quality Model, covering Functional Suitability, Performance Efficiency, Interaction Capability, Reliability, and Security.

#### Data Collection and Privacy

This study adheres to **Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012)** and its Implementing Rules and Regulations:

- **Facial Data:** Your face will be converted into a 512-dimensional numerical vector (face embedding). **No raw images, photographs, or video recordings of your face will be stored.** The embedding is an abstract numerical representation.
- **Personal Information:** Your name, email, and role designation will be collected solely for account creation and system operation.
- **Survey Responses:** Your feedback will be collected and used only for the evaluation of the system within this capstone study. Your responses will be treated with confidentiality.
- **Data Retention:** Upon completion of the study and final defense, all biometric data (face embeddings) will be permanently deleted from the database.
- **Access Control:** Only the researchers and authorized faculty advisers will have access to the collected data.

#### Voluntary Participation

Your participation is **entirely voluntary**. You may choose not to participate or withdraw at any time without any consequence. If you withdraw, all data collected from you will be promptly deleted.

#### Risks and Benefits

- **Risks:** The study involves minimal risk. Face embeddings are numerical data that cannot be reconstructed into facial images. No behavioral surveillance or performance evaluation is conducted through the system.
- **Benefits:** Your participation will help evaluate the viability of a low-cost, contactless attendance monitoring system for classroom use. Your expert feedback will directly contribute to the refinement of the system and the completion of an academic research project.
